    # Privacy

We keep it simple:
- We don’t sell your info.
- We only use contact details to communicate about Fight for Manhood.
- We collect limited, first-party traffic information so we can understand which pages are useful and whether outreach efforts are reaching people. This includes the page viewed, time, general country or state, referring website, campaign tags, device category, and a random session identifier.
- We do not store IP addresses, names, email addresses, advertising identifiers, or persistent visitor IDs in our traffic records. The tracker honors browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals.
- If you want your data removed, contact us and we will remove it.
